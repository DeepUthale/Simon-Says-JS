How to play

  • Press Start (or Enter) to begin.
  • Watch the glowing sequence and listen to the tones.
  • Repeat the sequence using clicks or 1–4.
  • Each level adds one new step.
  • Strict mode: one mistake ends the game.
  • Hint: replays the sequence once, but costs your streak.
